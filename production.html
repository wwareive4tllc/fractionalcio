What we do

Full-service production, from system design through load-out. You provide the venue, power, internet, and your program. We handle everything between the stage and the stream.

Technical direction

A lead video engineer directing the production live — switching, ISO recording, monitoring signal and stream health, and troubleshooting in real time so your team can focus on the program.

Multi-camera video

Cinema cameras and remote-operated PTZ, mixed live through a broadcast switcher. Wireless video transmission where cable runs aren't practical.

Livestreaming

Simultaneous delivery to your platforms — YouTube, Facebook, custom RTMP destinations — with redundant encoding and monitored bitrate.

Graphics & presentation

Dedicated operator running lower thirds, lyrics, scripture, sermon slides, and program graphics in sync with the live production.

Creative production

Event branding, motion graphics, bumper videos, and recap content — built before the event and delivered ready to air.

Recording & delivery

ISO recording of every camera plus the program feed, captured to SSD and delivered after the event for archive, rebroadcast, or editing.

Remote & virtual production

Full production directed remotely, with hosts and participants in different cities. We've run a two-day convention from Phoenix with the host in Cincinnati and an audience spread across two time zones — no travel required, no gear to ship.

Hybrid production

In-room and online audiences served at once, including coordination with your existing on-site production team or an on-grounds vendor. We've produced a national convention remotely alongside a separate company working the floor — two teams, two cities, one broadcast.
